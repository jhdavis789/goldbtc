How to hold gold

The inclusion question is given. This note chooses the vehicle — benchmark, custody, fee, tax, roll — for a US-taxable investor with a ten-year horizon.

Executive summary

  • Universe (AUM >$10B): GLD $157.6B · IAU $73.4B · GLDM $31.9B · PHYS $17.4B. Everything sub-scale (SGOL $8B, IAUM $6B, AAAU $2.8B, OUNZ ~$2B, BAR $1B) is mechanically equivalent to one of the four — same LBMA PM tracking, similar JPM or ICBC custody — and dropped here for clarity.
  • Wrapper, not roll, is the binding choice: spot grantor-trust ETF beats COMEX GC by ~60 bps/yr after-tax over ten years; §1256 annual mark-to-market is the disqualifying feature, regardless of the trailing-12m +231.7 bps GC backwardation.
  • Index: LBMA Gold Price PM auction is the only routable single-asset gold benchmark. GLD, IAU, GLDM all use it directly. PHYS uses Bloomberg GOLDS Comdty (continuous wholesale mid). Bloomberg Gold Subindex (12.46% weight in BCOM) and S&P GSCI Gold (7.24% in GSCI) are multi-commodity components — nothing in the >$10B set tracks them.
  • Three grantor-trust ETFs (GLD, IAU, GLDM) sit inside a ~17 bps/yr fee band on identical mechanics. GLDM at 10 bp ER is the cost minimum; IAU at 25 bp and GLD at 40 bp are dominated on fee alone within the JPM-custody family.
  • GLD's only differentiator is dual HSBC + JPM custody. That premium does not defend the 30 bps/yr gap to GLDM at any custody discount the bull side could support.
  • PHYS is a closed-end bullion trust at 35 bp MER, Royal Canadian Mint vault — only non-JPM, non-ICBC custody in the >$10B set. With the QEF election (Form 8621), routes to equity-LTCG character not 28% collectibles. Offset by a 93 bps/yr trailing-10y P/D drag and a persistent −2.4% discount to NAV.
  • Out of scope: inclusion sizing, royalty overlay, portfolio role. Settled by the verdict.

Spot grantor-trust ETF

Pros
  • Indefinite tax deferral until disposition; gain compounds on the full pre-tax balance.
  • NAV calibrated daily to LBMA PM; 5y observed slip-beyond-fee is a tight 10–17 bps.
  • 1.0 bp spread on GLD/GLDM; AP creation/redemption keeps P/D within ±10 bps.
  • Any sleeve size; no roll, no margin, no contract calendar.
Cons
  • 28% collectibles + 3.8% NIIT = 31.8% top bracket on disposition.
  • No collateral yield.
  • Custody concentrated in one London vault entity (JPM, ICBC Std, or HSBC).
  • Sponsor-fee level can rise (no fee floor in any prospectus).

COMEX GC / MGC futures

Pros
  • §1256 60/40 caps federal blended rate at ~26.8% — below the 28% collectibles ceiling.
  • ~23× notional leverage on GC; freed collateral earns T-bills at the front-end rate.
  • §1256(c) three-year loss carryback against prior §1256 gains.
  • Trailing-12m roll: +231.7 bps tailwind (RR5) — tradeable by short-horizon holders.
  • Deepest futures liquidity in the gold complex; GC top-of-book ~0.2 bp at $330k notional.
Cons
  • Mandatory year-end MTM under §1256(a)(1) destroys 10y deferral; after-tax ~60 bps/yr behind ETF, regime-invariant.
  • Six rolls per year; margin calls possible in stress (10–15% intraday move can force a same-day wire).
  • Notional coarse for individual sleeves; MGC carries ≈18 bps/yr spread tax (2–4 bp × 6 rolls).
  • Operational stack: futures-enabled brokerage, Form 6781, margin monitoring.

Commentary

The current regime makes this counterintuitive. Trailing-12m GC vs LBMA PM is +231.7 bps/yr (RR5); trailing-24m is +102.4 bps. A 10+y holder captures any one year's tailwind as taxable income and recompounds from a reduced base — the wrapper math is regime-invariant.

For 10+y US-taxable buy-and-hold, spot grantor-trust ETF dominates.

Synthesis

The wrapper is binding, not the roll. Spot grantor-trust ETF beats COMEX futures by ~60 bps/yr after-tax over a 10y compound, regime-invariant. Benchmark collapses to LBMA PM — Bloomberg and S&P GSCI gold sub-indices are 7%–12% gold-weighted inside diluted parents.

Of the four in-scope vehicles, three (GLD, IAU, GLDM) sit on identical LBMA-PM mechanics under JPM-related London custody and resolve on fee. GLDM at 10 bp delivers $174,000 on $100,000 over 10y at 8% gross — best after-tax in the JPM-custody family. IAU pays an unjustified 15 bp premium ($172k terminal); GLD pays 30 bp for dual HSBC + JPM custody that doesn't defend the gap ($171k terminal). PHYS sits outside this stack — Royal Canadian Mint custody, closed-end, 35 bp MER plus 93 bps/yr trailing 10-y discount drag — and is only competitive after a Form-8621 QEF election that routes gains to equity-LTCG character.
