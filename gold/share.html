Read

Gold's share of equities tracks a clear two-cycle pattern. The 2001–2011 secular gold bull pushed gold-vs-equities from ~5% at the 2000 equity peak to ~17% at the 2012 commodity-cycle high, after which the share halved during the 2013–2018 equity bull. The 2020–2024 leg is structurally different: gold mcap climbed alongside (not against) equities, with the central-bank purchase regime adding to demand outside private investor flows.

Against the broader financial-asset base, gold's share is more compressed and slower-moving — 3–6% for most of the period. The 2024 reading at 6.1% sits near the upper end of the post-1980 range without exceeding it, which is the more honest read of "where is gold positioned" than any equity-only ratio.
