Read

Against equities alone, gold's share has cycled in a 5–20% band over the last 35 years — ~16% in 1990 (pre-equity-bull), ~5% at the 2000 equity peak, ~17% at the 2012 commodity peak, ~11% at the 2021 equity peak. The current 19.9% reading is back near the 2012 cycle high. That's a tape-driven ratio; equity drawdowns and gold rallies both push it up.

Against the FSB total global financial assets base, gold's share is much smaller and slower-moving: roughly 1–4% across the full sample, with the current snapshot at 5.6% sitting at the high end of the post-1990 range. The denominator captures the bank balance sheets and pension reserves that equities + debt securities alone miss — at ~$550T it's roughly 2× the SIFMA equities + debt aggregate, which is why the share line is roughly half what an equities-only read implies. For a "where is gold sized relative to global financial wealth" view, this is the cleaner number.
