Stock-to-Flow (S2F)
"PlanB" (pseudonymous)
 Scarcity ratio 2021-cycle model called ~$100k–$288k; high six figures on scarcity alone Regresses price on the stock-to-flow ratio (existing supply ÷ annual new issuance). Each halving doubles S2F, producing step-changes in modeled value. The S2FX variant places BTC on a cross-asset scarcity line with gold and silver. Hugely influential 2019–21 but widely viewed as invalidated — price collapsed far below the model after 2021. Descriptive, not house-grade. "Modeling Bitcoin Value with Scarcity" 2019
Bitcoin Power Law
Giovanni Santostasi
 Power-law time regression ~$1M by ~2033 (~$10M by ~2045); a power-law corridor log(price) grows linearly with log(time since genesis), slope ~5.8 — treats Bitcoin as a network/physical phenomenon on a fixed power-law trajectory, defining a long-run support/resistance corridor with a dated ~$1M point around 2033. Strong historical fit; theoretical justification debated. A corridor with a 2033 midpoint, not a precise forecast. The Bitcoin Power Law Theory
Metcalfe network value
Timothy Peterson, Cane Island
 Network effect Probabilistic floors; ~$1.5M within 10yr (~2035, Jan 2026 update) Value scales with (active users)² (Metcalfe's Law), with adoption following a Gompertz / virus-spread curve. Yields "Lowest Price Forward" probabilistic price floors rather than upside targets. Published, peer-style methodology; floor-oriented (what BTC is unlikely to fall below). Cane Island / T. Peterson
Rainbow Chart
Blockchaincenter
 Logarithmic-regression bands Sentiment bands — no point target Fits a log-growth curve to price history and colors deviation bands from "fire sale" to "maximum bubble." A positioning/sentiment visual layered on the long-run log trend. Popular heuristic; explicitly not a valuation model (author's own disclaimer). Bitcoin Rainbow Chart
On-chain valuation
MVRV · NVT · Realized Cap · Thermocap
 On-chain ratios Relative cycle signals, not absolute targets Compare market cap to realized cap (MVRV), to transaction volume (NVT), or to cumulative miner revenue (Thermocap) to gauge over/undervaluation within a cycle and flag tops/bottoms. Timing & relative-value tools — not long-term price forecasts. Glassnode / Coin Metrics / Willy Woo
Cost of production
Energy-value / miner breakeven
 Production-cost floor A moving floor that tracks energy + hardware cost/coin The marginal cost to mine a coin (electricity + rig depreciation) acts as a soft price floor; "energy value" models translate hash-rate and power cost into an implied fair value. Floor logic; contested as an upper-bound valuation tool. Cambridge CBECI / energy-value models
Equation of Exchange (MV=PQ)
Burniske; Bank of England; Weber
 Medium-of-exchange / transactional utility Wide range by use-case: ~$34k (card-network) → multi-$M (world money) Treats BTC as money, not a stock: project addressable on-chain transactional volume (PQ), estimate velocity V (adjusting for HODLed supply), solve monetary base M = PQ/V, divide by supply, and discount future utility back at a high early-stage rate (~30–50%). Orthogonal to scarcity/network-size — can yield a LOW value even with fixed supply if velocity is high. A whole distinct valuation school. BoE illustrative outputs: illicit-only ~$19k; card-network ~$34k; all electronic payments ~$619k. BoE "Quantity theory of crypto" May 2026; Burniske

Reading the table. The cleanest divide is methodological, not numerical. At one extreme, a bloc of major houses publishes no allocation at all — Vanguard, Goldman ISG and UBS call crypto speculation with no cash flows or intrinsic value. Next sit the allocation-framework shops (BlackRock, Morgan Stanley, Bank of America) who answer "how big a sleeve, sized for risk" rather than "what's it worth." Everyone who does commit to a number uses one of a few engines — addressable-market build-ups (ARK use-case TAMs, VanEck's monetary model, Bitwise/Grayscale/WisdomTree/SSGA store-of-value share of gold), network-adoption math (Fidelity's Timmer), trend regression (Pantera's log-linear), flow/scenario decomposition (Citi, Standard Chartered, CoinShares), and a volatility-adjusted gold-parity formula (JPMorgan). Gold is the shared anchor for most — used three ways: a TAM to capture a share of, a qualitative debasement narrative, or a precise risk-capital divisor.

House view vs pontification. Several widely-quoted numbers are individual, not the firm's house view: Fidelity's ~$1M/2030 is Timmer's personal model (FDAS itself commits to no target); Galaxy's "BTC overtakes gold" is Novogratz's CEO remark, separate from Thorn's $250k/2027 research; Fink's $500–700k is an offhand Davos hypothetical. Section 2 collects the deliberately aggressive calls (Saylor's $13M, Draper's $250k) and the academic/on-chain models that aren't anyone's house view.
