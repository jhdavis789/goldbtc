Recommendation

40 FBTC / 60 IBIT at 25bp ER each, tracking CME CF BRRNY. Defections: 100% FBTC for concentration-averse · 100% IBIT for liquidity-bound · BTC Mini at 15bp for cost-bound. Futures-based vehicles (BITO, direct CME) lose by 400-900 bp/yr after-tax in every regime tested. GBTC at 150bp ER is strictly dominated.

Executive summary

  • 30-day median spreads (RR5, 2026-05-09): IBIT 1bp · FBTC 2bp · BITB/ARKB/GBTC 3bp · HODL/BTC Mini/EZBC 4bp · BRRR 9bp. The spread range is dwarfed by 15-25bp annual ER on every fund except HODL during its waiver.
  • Realized 28-month CAGR drag vs benchmark (RR4): HODL 30 / EZBC 32 / FBTC 51 / IBIT 52 / ARKB 54 / BTC Mini 58 / BITB 59 / BRRR 69 / GBTC 92 bps/yr. 62bp cross-fund spread compounds to ~6.4% over 10 years and is almost entirely an ER-less-waiver story, not an index methodology story.
  • Coinbase Custody = 85% of US spot ETF AUM, deployed-HHI 7,428 (~3× the DOJ "highly concentrated" 2,500 threshold). 40/60 FBTC/IBIT blend drops single-sponsor HHI from 10,000 (single-fund) to 5,200 at zero incremental ER. Anchorage is named as alternate on IBIT and BTC Mini but holds 0 BTC deployed (per Dec-2025 IBIT prospectus and reaffirmed in BTC Mini's Dec-2025 prospectus).
  • FBTC is the only major non-Coinbase deployed custodian — Fidelity Digital Assets under OCC NT charter, at the same 25bp ER as IBIT. The concentration-mitigating tilt is therefore free at the wrapper layer.
  • CME front-month vs spot basis (RR5): +2.88%/yr full-sample · +2.14% 2025 · +2.15% YTD 2026 — compressed ~45% from 2024 peak +3.90%/yr. RR3's +7.97% front-2nd slope is a separate statistic (curve carry a roller pays).
  • HODL is the only active fee waiver — 0% until 2026-07-31 or $2.5B AUM, whichever first. At $1.1B AUM the date binds first (~11 weeks remaining as of 2026-05-13); ER snaps to 20bp at expiry.
  • Premium/discount dispersion tightened ~27% post-SEC-2025-101 (in-kind C/R order, 2025-07-29): uniform σ ratio of 0.73 across all 9 ETFs, indicating system-wide AP-arbitrage efficiency improvement rather than fund-level effect.

Spot ETF — pros

  • Deferral preserved: 23.8% LTCG paid once at horizon. After-tax 10y CAGR 10.05% at 12% gross.
  • 1bp median spread (IBIT); ~28bp/yr total structural drag including 25bp ER.
  • Bankruptcy-remote trust under §541(d); holder is trust beneficiary, not unsecured creditor.
  • Roth IRA wrapper moots both the §408(m) collectibles tail and the LTCG realization event.

Spot ETF — cons

  • Custody concentration: deployed-HHI 7,428; Coinbase Custody holds 85% of complex AUM. The named-alternate roster is undeployed at the wrapper layer.
  • AUM concentration: IBIT alone is 54% of complex AUM ($54.2B / $89.4B); top three (IBIT + FBTC + GBTC) is ~88%; sub-scale funds (BRRR $0.43B, EZBC $0.41B) carry consolidation risk.
  • Administrator-sponsor conflicts: FBTC three-hat (admin + sponsor + custodian); HODL two-hat (MarketVector admin = VanEck affiliate); CF Benchmarks owned by Kraken parent.
  • Self-custody crossover at ~$1.4-1.9M position size — above that, ETF-only is dominated by an ETF + collaborative-custody barbell on TCO basis (Q06).
  • §408(m) collectibles tail latent for IRA distributions; no IRS revenue ruling on spot-BTC grantor-trust shares specifically.

Futures-based — pros

  • Tax-exempt accounts (Roth IRA, qualified plans, foundations) sidestep §1256 MTM entirely — the deferral-destruction kill chain does not apply at the holder level.
  • Capital-efficient overlay: ~40-45% SPAN margin ≈ $170k per direct-BTC contract on $410k notional; embedded ~2× effective leverage usable for overlay or short-hedge structures.
  • T-bill collateral on direct CME and inside BITO earns ~4%/yr in the current rate regime — offsets ~half the front-vs-spot basis at sustained rates.
  • CME Micro Bitcoin (MBT) at 0.10 BTC / ~$8.2k notional enables sub-$200k tactical sizing that spot-ETF round-trip cost makes inefficient.
  • CME-cleared, no custody-counterparty exposure to any single crypto firm; pure regulatory clearing-member risk.

Futures-based — cons

  • BITO realized 5.86%/yr drag in Regime C; trailing 12m 3.73%/yr reflects T-bill collateral kicker, not structural improvement.
  • Direct CME: holder rolls every ~30 days at +7.97%/yr front-2nd slope; ops burden and bid/ask not modeled.
  • No spot BTC at any layer — pure synthetic, no §541(d) protection.
  • BITO 95bp ER is the most expensive in the complex; curve carry it eats is then taxed annually on top.

Futures route wins only in: (i) tax-exempt accounts (§1256 MTM doesn't bind), (ii) capital-efficient overlay/hedge structures, (iii) sub-$200k tactical sizing via CME Micro MBT. None applies here.

For 10+y US-taxable buy-and-hold, spot ETF dominates.

Conclusion

Synthesis

Tax code decides wrapper; §1256 annual MTM eliminates the deferral that compounds over 10y. Within the spot complex, tracking error is index-agnostic (all 9 cluster at 20.3-21.3% annualized) — the differentiator is ER less waiver. FBTC + IBIT pair at 25bp across OCC NT and NYDFS Article-III charters: diversification at zero incremental cost. Flips the call: deployed Anchorage migration disclosed in any IBIT/Mini 10-Q, or sub-10bp ER from a non-Coinbase custodian.
