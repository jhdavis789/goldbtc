Bitcoin expression for a US-taxable 10-year sleeve

As of 2026-05-13 2 rounds · 5 research fills Verdict: debate2/verdict/verdict.md
Recommendation

40 FBTC / 60 IBIT at 25bp ER each, tracking CME CF BRRNY. Defections: 100% FBTC for concentration-averse · 100% IBIT for liquidity-bound. GBTC at 150bp ER is strictly dominated for new money — held only by tax-stranded incumbents. Futures-based vehicles (BITO, direct CME) lose by 400-900 bp/yr after-tax in every regime tested.

Executive summary

  • Universe (AUM >$10B, May 2026): IBIT $65.4B · FBTC $15.0B · GBTC $11.9B. Sub-scale spot ETFs (BTC Mini $4.2B, BITB $3.0B, ARKB $3.0B, HODL $1.3B, BTCO $0.5B, EZBC $0.5B, BRRR $0.5B) collectively hold ~14% of complex AUM and offer no structural advantage over the three at-scale wrappers.
  • Indices in scope: CME CF BRRNY (IBIT, FBTC) — UK-BMR-authorised, 8-venue VWMP, 15:00–16:00 NY, 10% outlier rule. CoinDesk Bitcoin Benchmark Rate (GBTC, since 2026-04-01, replacing the legacy CoinDesk XBX) — 11-venue volume-weighted with stale-quote penalties. Both are mature, audited, and behave identically over weekly windows; methodology is not a differentiator at horizon.
  • 30-day median spreads (RR5): IBIT 1bp · FBTC 2bp · GBTC 3bp. The spread range is dwarfed by the ER gap between 25bp (IBIT, FBTC) and 150bp (GBTC).
  • Realized 28-month CAGR drag vs benchmark (RR4): FBTC 51 · IBIT 52 · GBTC 92 bps/yr. The 40bp/yr GBTC gap is pure ER differential, not index methodology — compounds to ~5% over 10y.
  • Coinbase Custody = 85% of US spot ETF AUM, deployed-HHI 7,428 (~3× the DOJ "highly concentrated" 2,500 threshold). In the >$10B set, both IBIT and GBTC use CCTC sole; only FBTC sits at Fidelity Digital Assets (OCC NT). 40/60 FBTC/IBIT blend drops single-sponsor HHI from 10,000 to 5,200 at zero incremental ER. Anchorage is named as alternate on IBIT but holds 0 BTC deployed (Dec-2025 prospectus).
  • FBTC is the only non-Coinbase deployed custodian in the >$10B set — Fidelity Digital Assets under OCC NT charter, at the same 25bp ER as IBIT. The concentration-mitigating tilt is free at the wrapper layer.
  • GBTC is in scope by AUM, dominated by economics. 150bp ER vs 25bp at IBIT/FBTC compounds to ~14% drag at 10y. Held only by tax-stranded incumbents; Grayscale's explicit dual-vehicle design routes new flow to the BTC Mini sibling.
  • CME front-month vs spot basis (RR5): +2.88%/yr full-sample · +2.15% YTD 2026 — compressed ~45% from 2024 peak +3.90%/yr. Futures-based wrappers still lose by 400–900 bp/yr after-tax under §1256 annual MTM.

Spot ETF — pros

  • Deferral preserved: 23.8% LTCG paid once at horizon. After-tax 10y CAGR 10.05% at 12% gross.
  • 1bp median spread (IBIT); ~28bp/yr total structural drag including 25bp ER.
  • Bankruptcy-remote trust under §541(d); holder is trust beneficiary, not unsecured creditor.
  • Roth IRA wrapper moots both the §408(m) collectibles tail and the LTCG realization event.

Spot ETF — cons

  • Custody concentration: within the >$10B set, CCTC holds ~83.8% of AUM (IBIT + GBTC); 3-fund HHI ≈ 5,020. The broader 10-fund complex HHI is 7,428 — both are >2× the DOJ "highly concentrated" threshold. The named-alternate roster (Anchorage on IBIT) is undeployed at the wrapper layer.
  • AUM concentration: IBIT alone is 70.9% of the in-scope $92.3B AUM. Outside the filter, sub-scale funds (BTCO $0.53B, EZBC $0.49B, BRRR $0.49B) carry consolidation risk that wouldn't affect IBIT/FBTC/GBTC holders directly but is a wrapper-layer fragility signal.
  • Administrator-sponsor conflicts: FBTC three-hat (admin + sponsor + custodian); CF Benchmarks owned by Kraken parent.
  • Self-custody crossover at ~$1.4-1.9M position size — above that, ETF-only is dominated by an ETF + collaborative-custody barbell on TCO basis (Q06).
  • §408(m) collectibles tail latent for IRA distributions; no IRS revenue ruling on spot-BTC grantor-trust shares specifically.

Futures-based — pros

  • Tax-exempt accounts (Roth IRA, qualified plans, foundations) sidestep §1256 MTM entirely — the deferral-destruction kill chain does not apply at the holder level.
  • Capital-efficient overlay: ~40-45% SPAN margin ≈ $170k per direct-BTC contract on $410k notional; embedded ~2× effective leverage usable for overlay or short-hedge structures.
  • T-bill collateral on direct CME and inside BITO earns ~4%/yr in the current rate regime — offsets ~half the front-vs-spot basis at sustained rates.
  • CME Micro Bitcoin (MBT) at 0.10 BTC / ~$8.2k notional enables sub-$200k tactical sizing that spot-ETF round-trip cost makes inefficient.
  • CME-cleared, no custody-counterparty exposure to any single crypto firm; pure regulatory clearing-member risk.

Futures-based — cons

  • BITO realized 5.86%/yr drag in Regime C; trailing 12m 3.73%/yr reflects T-bill collateral kicker, not structural improvement.
  • Direct CME: holder rolls every ~30 days at +7.97%/yr front-2nd slope; ops burden and bid/ask not modeled.
  • No spot BTC at any layer — pure synthetic, no §541(d) protection.
  • BITO 95bp ER is the most expensive in the complex; curve carry it eats is then taxed annually on top.

Futures route wins only in: (i) tax-exempt accounts (§1256 MTM doesn't bind), (ii) capital-efficient overlay/hedge structures, (iii) sub-$200k tactical sizing via CME Micro MBT. None applies here.

For 10+y US-taxable buy-and-hold, spot ETF dominates.

Conclusion

Synthesis

Tax code decides wrapper; §1256 annual MTM eliminates the deferral that compounds over 10y. Within the >$10B set, index methodology is not differentiating — IBIT/FBTC use CME CF BRRNY (8-venue VWMP), GBTC uses the CoinDesk Bitcoin Benchmark Rate (11-venue, post-2026-04-01), and the realized 28m drag (FBTC 51 / IBIT 52 / GBTC 92 bp/yr) maps cleanly to ER less waiver. FBTC + IBIT pair at 25bp across OCC NT (Fidelity Digital Assets) and NYDFS Article-III (Coinbase) charters: diversification at zero incremental cost. Flips the call: deployed Anchorage migration disclosed in any IBIT 10-Q, or sub-10bp ER from a non-Coinbase custodian at scale.
